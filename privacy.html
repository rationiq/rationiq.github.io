RATION-IQ PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 6, 2026

RATION-IQ ("the Application," "we," "us," or "our") provides nutritional tracking, food analysis, and fitness support tools for users, including military personnel, first responders, and fitness enthusiasts.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the Application, how it is used, and the choices available to users. By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

OWNERSHIP INFORMATION

RATION-IQ is operated by a privately owned entity based in the United States of America. The Application is developed, maintained, and administered from the United States.

DEFINITIONS

  • Application: The mobile application known as RATION-IQ.
  • Device: Any smartphone, tablet, or other device used to access the Application.
  • Personal Data: Information that identifies or can reasonably be associated with an individual.
  • Health Data: Information voluntarily entered by the user relating to nutrition, body measurements, fitness metrics, or physical characteristics.
  • User Content: Photos, images, food entries, text, or other content submitted by the user.
  • Website: https://rationiq.github.io/

INFORMATION PROCESSED BY THE APPLICATION

Account Information & Local Storage: Health and fitness information entered into RATION-IQ is stored locally on the user's device and remains under the user's control. RATION-IQ does not maintain centralized databases containing user body measurements, food logs, meal histories, fitness scores, or nutrition records.

Food Images: Users may submit food images for nutritional analysis. Food images are processed solely to provide the nutritional analysis requested by the user. They are not used for identity recognition, facial recognition, biometric identification, advertising, user profiling, AI model training, machine learning development, or marketing purposes. RATION-IQ does not retain food images for future AI training or model-development purposes.

HOW INFORMATION IS USED

RATION-IQ uses information solely to provide Application functionality requested by the user, including nutritional analysis, calorie estimation, meal tracking, fitness calculations, profile management, app integrity, and customer support. RATION-IQ does not use user information for advertising purposes.

LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

RATION-IQ is designed using a local-first architecture. User profiles, body measurements, fitness metrics, food logs, meal histories, nutrition records, and related information are stored locally on the user's device. The Application is designed to minimize dependence on centralized storage and external systems.

INFORMATION PROCESSING AND SHARING

RATION-IQ does not sell Personal Data or Health Data.

RATION-IQ does not use Personal Data or Health Data for advertising.

RATION-IQ does not share Health Data with data brokers.

RATION-IQ does not use Health Data for AI model training or machine learning development.

RATION-IQ does not monetize Health Data.

When a user requests nutritional analysis, the selected food image is transmitted solely for the purpose of generating the nutrition analysis requested by the user. Other than this temporary transmission, RATION-IQ does not share user food images or Health Data with third parties.

USER CONTENT RIGHTS

By submitting User Content, you grant RATION-IQ a limited, non-exclusive, and temporary license to process User Content solely to provide app functionality requested by the user. No ownership rights are transferred to RATION-IQ. RATION-IQ does not publicly display user content.

DATA RETENTION & LOCATION

Information stored locally on the user's device remains available until deleted by the user through the Application's deletion functions or by removing the Application from the device. RATION-IQ does not maintain centralized repositories of user food logs, meal histories, body measurements, fitness records, or stored food images. RATION-IQ does not transfer, sell, distribute, or maintain user information across multiple geographic regions.

DATA DELETION

RATION-IQ users can request deletion of their data at any time.

To request deletion, email us at mdtinvest@protonmail.com with the subject "RATION-IQ Data Deletion Request" and include the email address or other information needed to identify your request.

Upon receiving a valid request, we will delete the personal data associated with the request that we control, except where retention is required by law or necessary for legitimate legal or security purposes.

Data is stored locally on the user's device, which can also be deleted using the "Delete My Profile (Data)" function within the RATION-IQ app Profile Setup Module.

Subscription and purchase records processed through Google Play or Adapty may be retained by those services according to their respective policies and legal requirements.

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on applicable law and your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete information stored within the Application, withdraw consent, and request information regarding how the Application handles your data. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom may also have the right to submit complaints to their local data protection authority.

SECURITY

RATION-IQ uses the security mechanisms provided by the operating system and application sandbox to help prevent unauthorized access to locally stored information. While reasonable safeguards are employed, no software or device can guarantee absolute security.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

RATION-IQ is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. The Application is designed for use by adults and does not knowingly solicit information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the Application, discontinue use and remove the Application from the device. Contact: mdtinvest@protonmail.com

THIRD-PARTY LINKS

The Application may provide links to third-party resources for educational fitness training purposes. When a user opens an external link, they leave the RATION-IQ environment. RATION-IQ does not control the operation, content, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites or services.
