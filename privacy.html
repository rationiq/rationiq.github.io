PRIVACY POLICY

 

Effective Date: January 6, 2026

 

RATION-IQ ("the Application," "we," "us," or "our") provides nutritional tracking, food analysis, and fitness support tools for users, including military personnel, first responders, and fitness enthusiasts.

 

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the Application, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

 

By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

 

OWNERSHIP INFORMATION

 

RATION-IQ is operated by a privately owned entity based in the United States of America.

The Application is developed, maintained, and administered from the United States.

 

  1. DEFINITIONS

 

Application means the mobile application known as RATION-IQ.

Device means any smartphone, tablet, or other device used to access the Application.

Personal Data means information that identifies or can reasonably be associated with an individual.

Health Data means information voluntarily entered by the user relating to nutrition, body measurements, fitness metrics, or physical characteristics.

User Content means photos, images, food entries, text, or other content submitted by the user.

Website means https://rationiq.github.io/

You means the individual using the Application.

 

  1. INFORMATION PROCESSED BY THE APPLICATION

 

Account Information

The Application may process profile and health-related information voluntarily provided by the user, including body measurements, nutrition goals, and fitness preferences.

Local Storage Notice:

Health and fitness information entered into RATION-IQ is stored locally on the user's device and remains under the user's control.

RATION-IQ does not maintain centralized databases containing user body measurements, food logs, meal histories, fitness scores, or nutrition records.

Food Images:

Users may submit food images for nutritional analysis.

Food images are processed solely to provide the nutritional analysis requested by the user.

Food images are not used for:

• Identity recognition
• Facial recognition
• Biometric identification
• Advertising
• User profiling
• AI model training
• Machine learning development
• Marketing purposes

Food images are transmitted only as necessary to perform the requested nutritional analysis.

RATION-IQ does not retain food images for future AI training or model-development purposes.

User Content (Including Photos)

User Content may include:

• Food photographs submitted for nutritional analysis
• Nutrition and meal log entries
• User-generated text and notes

When a food photograph is submitted for analysis, the image is transmitted to an external processing service solely for the purpose of generating nutrition and food analysis results requested by the user.

 

  1. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

 

RATION-IQ uses information solely to provide Application functionality requested by the user, including:

• Nutritional analysis
• Calorie estimation
• Meal tracking
• Fitness calculations
• Profile management
• Application functionality
• App integrity and abuse prevention
• Customer support

RATION-IQ does not use user information for advertising purposes.

 

  1. LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

 

RATION-IQ is designed using a local-first architecture.

User profiles, body measurements, fitness metrics, food logs, meal histories, nutrition records, and related information are stored locally on the user's device.

The Application is designed to minimize dependence on centralized storage and external systems.

 

5.     INFORMATION PROCESSING AND SHARING

 

RATION-IQ does not sell Personal Data.

RATION-IQ does not sell Health Data.

RATION-IQ does not use Personal Data or Health Data for advertising.

RATION-IQ does not maintain centralized databases containing user food logs, meal histories, body measurements, fitness metrics, profile information, or food images.

User information is stored locally on the user's device and remains under the user's control.

When a user requests nutritional analysis, the selected food image is transmitted solely for the purpose of generating the nutrition analysis requested by the user. The image is not used by RATION-IQ for advertising, profiling, AI model training, machine learning development, or marketing purposes.

RATION-IQ does not retain, archive, catalog, or build datasets from food images submitted for analysis.

Other than temporary transmission of food images to an external processing service solely for user-requested analysis, RATION-IQ does not share user food images or Health Data with third parties.

 

  1. HEALTH DATA USE LIMITATIONS

 

RATION-IQ:

• Does not sell Health Data
• Does not use Health Data for advertising
• Does not share Health Data with data brokers
• Does not use Health Data for AI model training
• Does not monetize Health Data

Health Data remains stored locally on the user's device.

  1. DATA RETENTION

 

Information stored locally on the user's device remains available until deleted by the user through the Application's deletion functions or by removing the Application from the device.

  1. DATA LOCATION AND STORAGE

 

RATION-IQ is designed using a local-first architecture. User profiles, body measurements, fitness metrics, nutrition records, food logs, meal histories, and stored images remain on the user's device and are not maintained in centralized databases operated by RATION-IQ.

RATION-IQ does not transfer, sell, distribute, or maintain user information across multiple geographic regions as part of its operation. The Application is designed to minimize the collection, storage, and external transmission of user information.
