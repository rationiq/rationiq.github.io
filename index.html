KNOW YOUR NUMBER BEFORE TEST DAY: THE 2026 WAIST-TO-HEIGHT STANDARD

With updated Army Fitness Test combat standards effective 2026, RATION-IQ introduces Performance Path Intelligence. Since July 7, 2026, the Army measures body composition by waist-to-height ratio only. Your waist at the navel divided by your height must be under 0.55. No AFT score exempts a Soldier.

  • A Soldier who is 70 inches tall needs a waist under 38.5 inches.
  • Multiply your height by 0.55 to find yours.
  • RATION-IQ does the math and shows pass or fail.
  • Optimize food intake.
  • Reduce uncertainty.
  • Sources: Army Directive 2025-06 (AFT) and Army Directive 2026-13 (body composition). Army policy can change, so confirm with your unit.

An NCO Scenario Case Study

Sergeant Miller is going for a meal. Before stepping away from the chow line, he pauses. He scans his tray with his phone RATION-IQ app.

Within seconds, RATION-IQ breaks the meal down into clear, actionable data - calories, protein, carbohydrates, sodium - without manual logging. A visual alert flags excess sugar and empty calories from a cake dessert.

RATION-IQ visual overlay instantly classifies the meal:

  • Green: Lean protein and complex carbohydrates, performance fuel locked in PROCEED.
  • Yellow: Elevated sodium (or other), proceed with CAUTION.
  • Red: High sugar cake, AVOID.

Sergeant Miller makes a correction. The cake goes back. The rest stays. As the item leaves the tray, the numbers update in real time. His fuel profile shifts into range. The nutritional risk is identified before the meal is consumed.

By the time he sits down to eat, Sergeant Miller is not guessing. He has objective nutritional data about what he is fueling with - and more importantly, what he avoided. That food decision improved the nutritional quality of the meal before training and testing day.

COMMAND CENTER: TRAIN THE EVENT, NOT THE DISTRACTION

RATION-IQ does not bury you in generic exercises. The AFT has five events, and the best way to improve at an event is to practice that event.

The Command Center shows each one:

  • 3-Repetition Maximum Deadlift
  • Hand-Release Push-Up
  • Sprint-Drag-Carry
  • Plank
  • 2-Mile Run

Tap an event to watch the official U.S. Army instructional video for it on YouTube, and learn the correct technique from the source.

You will be tested on these five events, so train these five events. Want a stronger deadlift? Practice the deadlift. Want more push-ups? Practice push-ups the way the standard requires. RATION-IQ keeps you focused on where your score is earned.
